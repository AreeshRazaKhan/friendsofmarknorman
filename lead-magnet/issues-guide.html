Grounded leadership for Oregon.

[ issue 01 / education ]

Strong basics. Real skills. Parents in the loop.

Oregon students deserve schools that prepare them for adult life.

Mark believes education should focus first on reading, writing, mathematics, science, civics, discipline, critical thinking, and career readiness. Students should graduate with the skills they need for college, trades, military service, or the workforce.

Mark’s concern is not with dedicated teachers who care deeply about their students. His concern is with a system that has too often lost focus on measurable academic achievement and clear accountability.

Mark’s education priorities include
  • Evidence-based reading instruction
  • Strong math fundamentals
  • Clear academic standards
  • Safe and orderly classrooms
  • Parent transparency and involvement
  • More vocational and technical pathways
  • Greater flexibility for local school districts
  • Funding connected to student achievement
“Teachers should be empowered to teach, parents should be respected as partners, and students should leave school prepared for real opportunity.”

[ issue 02 / public safety ]

Safe communities require accountability and compassion.

Public safety is one of the most basic responsibilities of government.

Families should feel safe at home. Children should feel safe walking to school. Seniors should feel safe using public transportation. Local businesses should feel confident investing in their communities.

Mark supports practical, balanced public safety policies that include strong law enforcement, accountability, community partnership, mental health resources, treatment access, and safe neighborhoods for everyone.

Mark’s public safety priorities include
  • Fully funding public safety
  • Improving law enforcement recruitment and retention
  • Competitive compensation and strong training
  • Strengthening community policing
  • Behavioral health and crisis-response partnerships
  • Improving coordination between agencies
  • Addressing addiction, homelessness, and repeat offenders
  • Supporting both effective policing and public trust
“Oregonians should not have to choose between compassion and safety. We need both.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 03 / small business ]

Easier to start, grow, and keep a business.

Small businesses are the backbone of Oregon’s economy.

Family-owned businesses, independent contractors, restaurants, farms, retailers, service providers, medical practices, and startups all face rising costs and increasing complexity.

As a small-business owner, Mark understands that small employers cannot simply absorb endless new costs. When the cost of doing business rises, it affects prices, hiring, wages, investment, and whether local businesses can keep their doors open.

Mark’s small-business priorities include
  • Reducing unnecessary regulations
  • Streamlining licensing, permitting, and approvals
  • Reviewing and limiting excessive fee increases
  • Access to startup capital and workforce training
  • Protecting contractors from burdensome mandates
  • Economic impact review before major new regulations
  • Targeted tax relief for small businesses
  • Encouraging reinvestment, expansion, and job creation
“Oregon’s economy will be strengthened by supporting entrepreneurship, rewarding hard work, encouraging innovation, and helping small businesses succeed.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 04 / animal welfare ]

Humane treatment. Responsible ownership. Practical solutions.

Animal welfare is deeply important to Mark.

As a veterinarian with more than 30 years of experience serving Oregon communities, Mark has seen both the best and worst of how animals are treated. He believes Oregon has a moral responsibility to treat animals humanely and ethically while also respecting responsible animal ownership.

Mark supports practical, science-based efforts that improve animal welfare without creating unnecessary harm for responsible families, veterinarians, farmers, ranchers, hunters, and pet owners.

Mark’s animal welfare priorities include
  • Reducing animal cruelty and neglect
  • Education around responsible ownership
  • Improving access to veterinary care
  • Supporting rescue organizations
  • Practical spay and neuter programs
  • Reducing pet overpopulation
  • Respecting farmers, ranchers, hunters, and pet owners
“Compassion for animals and respect for responsible animal ownership can, and should, exist together.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 05 / data centers & energy ]

Responsible innovation needs reliable energy and accountability.

Data centers are becoming a major infrastructure and economic issue for Washington County and Oregon.

Mark supports responsible data center development because technology, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and digital infrastructure are increasingly important to the modern economy. These facilities can support high-value industries and strengthen Oregon’s long-term competitiveness.

At the same time, growth must be responsible. Residents are right to ask questions about electricity demand, water consumption, land use, utility costs, local infrastructure, environmental impact, and tax fairness. Mark supports an all-of-the-above energy strategy — hydroelectric, natural gas, renewables, and next-generation nuclear power, especially Small Modular Reactor technology.

Mark’s data center priorities include
  • Clear standards for power generation
  • Water sustainability
  • Environmental stewardship
  • Infrastructure investment
  • Fair tax contribution
  • Protection for local communities
  • Reliable and affordable energy planning
“Oregon can support innovation, protect communities, and build reliable energy systems at the same time.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 06 / transportation & energy ]

Transportation and energy, grounded in reality.

Mark supports public transportation, but major investments should be based on actual rider behavior, financial sustainability, public safety, long-term maintenance, and measurable public benefit.

He believes Oregon should be honest about changing commuter patterns, infrastructure costs, energy demand, and the real impact of policies on household budgets.

Mark’s transportation and energy priorities include
  • Evaluating transportation projects on actual use and cost
  • Investing in infrastructure people actually use
  • Flexible and practical transit options
  • Protecting affordability for families and businesses
  • Supporting reliable energy generation
  • Balancing stewardship with economic reality
  • Avoiding costs without clear, measurable benefits
“Oregon needs transportation and energy policies focused on affordability, mobility, reliability, public safety, and results.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 07 / healthcare ]

Practical, patient-centered, responsible reform.

Mark understands the frustration many families feel with healthcare.

Costs are too high. Insurance bureaucracy is overwhelming. Healthcare workers are burned out. Too many families delay care because they are worried about affordability.

Mark does not support a government-run single-payer healthcare system for Oregon. He believes healthcare reform should focus on practical improvements that increase affordability, access, transparency, and patient choice.

Mark’s healthcare priorities include
  • Access to primary and preventive care
  • Less administrative burden on providers
  • Improving price transparency
  • Encouraging competition and innovation
  • Supporting independent medical practices
  • Stronger rural and community healthcare access
  • Expanding workforce training
  • Practical mental health and addiction treatment
  • Keeping decisions between patients and professionals
“Compassion must be paired with competence, sustainability, accountability, and honesty about costs and consequences.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 08 / veterans ]

Veterans deserve dignity, respect, and real support.

As a retired Navy veteran, Mark understands that military service leaves a lasting impact long after the uniform is removed.

Veterans bring leadership, technical skill, discipline, resilience, and experience to their communities. At the same time, too many veterans face challenges with healthcare, mental health, housing, employment, addiction, isolation, and complex government systems.

Mark’s veterans priorities include
  • Improving access to quality healthcare
  • Strengthening mental health services
  • Expanding addiction treatment access
  • Supporting housing assistance
  • Improving workforce training
  • Helping with career transition
  • Employment for veterans and military families
  • Stronger partnerships across agencies and providers
“Veterans fulfilled their commitment to this nation. The government and society should fulfill their commitment to them.”
Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com

[ issue 09 / local issues ]

Local problems need practical leadership.

Many people feel politically homeless.

Some are tired of division and hostility. Others are hesitant to speak openly because they fear being labeled, dismissed, or attacked.

Mark believes Oregon should remain a place where people of different backgrounds, faiths, viewpoints, and political beliefs can live and work together respectfully. He rejects hatred toward any group and believes the state legislature should focus on practical Oregon issues rather than being consumed by national ideological battles.

Mark’s focus includes
  • Affordability
  • Education
  • Public safety
  • Small business growth
  • Energy costs
  • Transportation
  • Housing
  • Government accountability
  • Respectful dialogue
  • Common-sense leadership
“People do not need to agree on everything to work together on the issues affecting Oregon families every day.”
