01

Meet Mark Norman.

I am a Navy veteran, veterinarian, and small-business owner running for Oregon House District 27.

My campaign is focused on grounded leadership, practical decisions, and everyday accountability. I am not approaching this campaign as a career politician. I bring experience from Navy service, veterinary medicine, small business, and years of listening to Oregon families, workers, employers, parents, and community members.

As a Navy veteran, I understand service, responsibility, discipline, and accountability as a part of my duty.

I spent decades serving families and caring for animals with compassion, science, and practical judgment as a veterinarian.

As a small-business owner, I understand how taxes, regulations, staffing, costs, and government decisions affect real people every day.

My position

I believe Oregon needs leadership that listens, solves problems, respects taxpayers, and focuses on results.

02

Why I’m running.

I’m running because Oregon families deserve better than excuses.

Too many families are carrying more pressure than they should. Everyday costs are rising. Housing is harder to afford. Utility bills are going up. Healthcare is expensive. Parents are concerned about schools. Small businesses are being squeezed. Public safety matters. Trust in government has been weakened by waste, excuses, and lack of accountability.

I believe House District 27 deserves leadership focused on real problems, not political noise.

Government should be practical, transparent, and accountable. Public programs should be measured by results. Families should not have to keep paying more while getting less. Parents should be informed. Communities should be safe. Small businesses should have room to grow.

My campaign is built around a simple idea.

My position

Oregon needs service, solutions, and accountability.

03
Affordability

Families need breathing room.

I believe costs should be manageable.

Too many Oregon households are adjusting their lives around rising expenses. Families are paying more for housing, groceries, utilities, healthcare, gas, insurance, and everyday needs.

State government should not make life harder through unnecessary taxes, fees, mandates, delays, or policies that raise costs.

I Support
  • Responsible state spending
  • Transparent budgets
  • Opposition to unnecessary tax and fee increases
  • Policies that help reduce everyday pressure
  • Housing solutions that remove unnecessary barriers
  • Support for small businesses that provide jobs and services
My position

Government should make it easier to live, work, raise a family, and build a future in Oregon — not harder.

04
Education

Strong schools, real opportunity.

I believe students need strong fundamentals and real preparation for life.

Schools should focus on reading, writing, math, science, discipline, career readiness, technical education, and skills that prepare students for college, trades, military service, or the workforce.

Parents should be informed and involved. Teachers should be supported. Classrooms should be safe, focused, and serious about student success.

I Support
  • High academic standards
  • Reading, math, science, and career readiness
  • Parent transparency and involvement
  • Safe and focused classrooms
  • Technical and career pathways
  • Funding directed toward student achievement
My position

Students deserve schools that prepare them for real opportunity, and parents deserve to know what is happening in the classroom.

Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com
05
Accountability

Taxpayers deserve results.

I believe the public deserves transparency.

Oregonians work hard for their money. When government spends public dollars, voters deserve to know where the money goes, who is responsible, and what results are being produced.

Government programs should be evaluated by outcomes, not by size. More spending is not the same as better results.

I Support
  • Independent review of public spending
  • Transparency across agencies
  • Clear reporting on program outcomes
  • Responsible use of taxpayer dollars
  • Programs measured by results, not promises
  • Accountability before asking taxpayers for more money
My position

Government should not be rewarded for failure. Taxpayers deserve answers, oversight, and results.

Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com
06
Public Safety

Safe communities matter.

I believe families, students, seniors, and small businesses should all feel safe.

Families should feel safe at home, students should feel safe at school, seniors should feel safe in their neighborhoods, and small businesses should feel safe opening their doors. Public safety is one of the basic responsibilities of government.

I support safe, stable communities, law enforcement recruitment, accountability, and practical policies that protect people while addressing serious problems like addiction, mental health, homelessness, and public disorder.

I Support
  • Safe and stable neighborhoods
  • Law enforcement recruitment and retention
  • Accountability in public safety systems
  • Real help for addiction and mental health challenges
  • Protection for families, seniors, students, and businesses
  • Policies that restore trust and stability
My position

Compassion and safety should work together. Oregon should help people in crisis without ignoring public disorder or leaving communities unprotected.

Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com
07
Small Business

Local employers need room to grow.

I believe small businesses are the backbone of local communities.

They create jobs, serve families, sponsor local events, support neighborhoods, and take real risks every day. They should not be treated like endless revenue sources for bigger government.

I understand the pressure small businesses face because I have lived it. Taxes, fees, permits, insurance, staffing, regulations, energy costs, and delays all affect whether local employers can survive and grow.

I Support
  • Reducing unnecessary burdens
  • Faster and more predictable permitting
  • Lower pressure from taxes and fees
  • A stronger business climate
  • Support for job creation
  • Policies that help local employers stay viable
My position

Oregon should support the people who build, hire, serve, and invest in local communities.

Mark Norman · Oregon HD-27 markfororegon.com
08

More priorities I care about.

My campaign is focused on practical issues that affect daily life across House District 27.

Healthcare

I believe healthcare should be easier to access, easier to understand, and more affordable. I support patient choice, transparency, provider support, mental health care, addiction treatment, and practical reform.

Energy

I believe Oregon needs reliable and affordable energy. Families and businesses should not be punished by policies that raise utility bills or weaken reliability.

Veterans

As a Navy veteran, I believe veterans deserve real support, not empty promises. I support systems that deliver practical help with healthcare, housing, mental health, employment, and transition services.

Animal Welfare

As a veterinarian, I care deeply about animal welfare. I support strong, practical, science-based protections against cruelty and neglect while respecting responsible ownership, agriculture, veterinary care, and lawful animal-related activities.

Local Communities

I believe major decisions should include local voices. Families, neighborhoods, small businesses, workers, and community leaders deserve to be heard before policies affect their lives.
